Fish detection ≠ fish identity

A frame-by-frame synthetic benchmark that separates finding fish-shaped objects from maintaining their tracker labels. Adjust the error scenario and inspect its consequences. These metrics refer only to invented ground-truth boxes and synthetic predictions.

Detection boxes are paired greedily in descending IoU with a one-to-one constraint. The displayed ID-change count compares assigned labels for the same synthetic fish in adjacent matched frames. It is not official HOTA, IDF1, TrackEval, or an independent tracking benchmark. When no predictions exist, precision is defined as zero for this teaching dashboard.
